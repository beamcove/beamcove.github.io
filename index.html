II. — The Charter

Three things we sail by.

Every ship needs a charter. Ours is short, on purpose — three pole stars to hold a course by when the work gets tangled.

N 01

Adventure

We build things we want to find out about. New mediums, new metaphors, the occasional left turn — kept honest by the next two values.

E 02

Integrity

We don't ship dishonest software. No dark patterns, no sneaky pricing, no tracking that makes us flinch. Quality you can sleep on.

S 03

Balance

Slow enough to make it good. Fast enough to make it real. The cove is meant to be a sustainable place to keep working from for a long time.
